# Your contact form gives you 40 emails a week. 38 are junk.

**An AI lead qualification bot** that does the opposite of a contact form: it *asks questions*.

It greets a visitor, asks the five questions that decide whether they're a real buyer — need, timeline, size, budget, decision power — answers their questions from your own site, then scores the conversation and messages you **only if the lead is hot**. Cold leads go into a daily digest instead of interrupting your day.

---

## The problem

A contact form gives you 40 emails a week and 38 of them are junk. A "book a demo" button gives you demos with people who will never buy. You spend your evenings separating the two — and the hot ones go cold while you do it.

## What it does

A chat widget on your site that qualifies the visitor for you, in conversation.

- **It asks the five questions** — the same ones you'd ask on the phone, woven into the chat one at a time instead of in a form.
- **It answers from your own site.** Hours, pricing, services — quoted from your pages, never invented.
- **It never asks twice.** If a visitor mentions "we're a team of 12" in passing, it notes it and moves on.
- **It scores every conversation 0-100** on need, timeline, budget, decision power, and fit.
- **Hot leads reach your phone instantly** — Telegram message with the score, the answers, and the transcript. Warm and cold go into a digest.
- **One line to install.** Any site — WordPress, Shopify, Wix, plain HTML.

## Who this is for

- **Service businesses and agencies** that get a lot of "just looking" inquiries and a few real buyers buried in them.
- **SaaS and consultancies** where a demo with the wrong person wastes an hour nobody gets back.
- **Any business whose inbox is a filter problem.**

## What it does not do

- **It does not book meetings.** It qualifies; you close.
- **It does not email leads for you.** The digest is for *you*, about them.
- **It reads text, not images.** If your pricing is a screenshot, it can't quote it.
