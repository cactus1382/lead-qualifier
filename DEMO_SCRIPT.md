# Demo script — 90 seconds, one take, phone is fine

**Setup (5 minutes before):** server running, a demo site ingested, widget loaded on a plain HTML page you control. Have a second browser tab open on `/api/demo/leads` (admin view) so you can flip to it at the end.

---

**0:00 — [camera on the screen, chat closed]**
"This is a website. There's no way to ask it anything. If I have a question at 11pm, I leave, and the business never finds out I was here."

*Click the bubble. The bot greets.*

**0:08**
"Now there is. I built a chat that qualifies the visitor — it asks the questions I'd ask if I were on the phone."

*Type: "We need a new booking system, we're a team of 14"*

**0:16**
"Watch what it does with that. It didn't just answer — it pulled out 'booking system' and '14 people' and it won't ask me those again."

*The bot asks about timeline.*

**0:22**
"It answers from the site's own content. Watch — nothing is invented."

*Type: "How much does it cost?"*

*The bot quotes the real price from the site.*

**0:30**
"That price came from the website. It cannot make one up — it quotes the page or it says it isn't certain. For a clinic quoting an allergen, that's the difference between a customer and a lawsuit."

*Answer the remaining questions quickly: "this quarter", "around $2k a month", "I decide".*

**0:45 — the score appears**
"And now — it scores the lead."

*The hot-lead message appears in the chat AND a Telegram notification lands on your phone. Hold the phone up to the camera.*

**0:55**
"That's a hot lead, 88 out of 100, with the score, the answers, and the reason — on my phone the second it finished the conversation. I didn't read it, filter it, or qualify it. It did."

**1:05**
"A cold lead doesn't do that. It goes into a digest so it doesn't waste my evening."

*Flip to the leads tab.*

**1:10**
"Here's every conversation, scored, with the transcript. This is what my inbox should have looked like all along."

**1:15 — [camera back to you]**
"Five questions, a score, and a ping when it's actually worth my time. That's the whole thing."

---

**If they ask "does it work on my site?"** — yes, one line of script, WordPress/Shopify/Wix/plain HTML.

**If they ask "what if it gives a wrong answer?"** — it can't invent a price. It answers from your own pages or says it isn't certain and hands you the lead.

**If they ask the price** — $400 done-for-you, live in 48 hours, and you don't pay until you watch it qualify a real conversation. $75 a month to keep it running. Cancel any time, you keep it.
